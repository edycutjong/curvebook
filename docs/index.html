    Scene 03 · The Form

    Pick a curve by its record

    The Form ranks every DBC config by its median first-ten-slot sniper share, the way a racing form lists a runner's last starts. Each config page reads its on-chain parameters back in plain words and shows the fee a slot-0 buyer would pay.

    1. Ranked only with real form: at least 20 complete windows from at least 5 distinct creators.
    2. Ties are declared, not hidden: ranks share a place when their 90% bootstrap intervals overlap.
    3. Farming shows: the top creator's share of launches is printed beside every config.
    4. Plain words from the chain: "Flat 0.25% fee from the first slot. No anti-sniper schedule."

    Five parts, one flow

    Decode the buyer

    EvtSwap2 has no payer field, so every buy is paired with the swap instruction that emitted it, even when an aggregator routes it. Failed transactions, the legacy EvtSwap twin and v1 transactions are all handled.

    Finalize from the ledger

    Windows are re-read from confirmed signatures after slot +42, so a dropped fork or a stream gap can't shorten one.

    Rank honestly

    ≥ 20 windows from ≥ 5 creators to rank; ties when 90% bootstrap intervals overlap.

    Scene 08 · Questions a judge would ask

    Ask us anything

    Six answers, each with the file that backs it.

    Is this real mainnet data?

    Yes. Every window is decoded from Meteora DBC transactions on Solana mainnet since capture start. pnpm proof re-fetches 20 sampled buys from mainnet and checks slot, payer and amount: 20/20 match (DEMO.md §2).

    What isn't on mainnet yet?

    The three presets are live on mainnet with one own launch each (DEMO.md §1b), and their fees are claimed there. The royalty router that splits those fees author/treasury runs on devnet, with every step on the explorer (DEMO.md §3b); until it moves, mainnet presets name the author's wallet as fee claimer.

    What counts as an "outside" buy?

    Any buy whose paying key is not the pool creator's. The payer comes from the DBC swap instruction that emitted the event, so aggregator-routed buys are attributed too. Creator-funded wallets count as outside, and the page says so.

    Can a launchpad farm a good rank?

    Not cheaply. A config needs at least 20 complete windows from at least 5 creators, the top creator's share of launches is printed, and ranks tie when the 90% intervals overlap.

    What does the live index run on?

    RPC Fast: a Yellowstone gRPC stream of every DBC transaction, with from_slot replay after a reconnect or redeploy, and every ten-slot window re-read from confirmed signatures. Without a gRPC endpoint, or if the provider stops accepting the key, it falls back to public RPC (logsSubscribe), so anyone can run it with no paid keys.

    Can my launchpad use it without your UI?

    Yes. Call buildLaunchTx from your own backend (examples/launch-on-preset.ts). The preset's royalty is enforced by its on-chain config, not by our frontend.
